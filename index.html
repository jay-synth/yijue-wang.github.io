About

I am a Research Scientist specializing in reliable, privacy-preserving, and human-centered AI. My work spans machine learning, multimodal AI, privacy, and recommendation systems, with a focus on translating research advances into trustworthy and scalable AI solutions.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Connecticut, where I was advised by Prof. Sanguthevar Rajasekaran. My doctoral research focused on the privacy, reliability, and efficiency of machine learning systems.

I have worked on applied AI research at Meta Platforms and Samsung Research America, contributing to large-scale machine learning, multimodal modeling, and recommendation systems. I am particularly interested in opportunities that connect innovative AI research with practical products and meaningful user impact.
