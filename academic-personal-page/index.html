About

I am a Research Scientist working on building reliable, privacy-preserving, and human-centered AI systems. My research spans machine learning, multimodal AI, privacy, and recommendation systems, with a particular interest in developing AI systems that can learn from complex real-world data while remaining trustworthy and responsible.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Connecticut, where I was advised by Prof. Sanguthevar Rajasekaran. My doctoral research focused on the privacy and efficiency of machine learning models, including understanding how models can inadvertently reveal information about their training data, developing methods to mitigate such privacy risks, and improving the efficiency of modern AI models.

More broadly, my research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, privacy, and recommendation systems. I am particularly interested in reliable and privacy-aware AI assistants, multimodal learning, and learning from human behavior.

I have also worked on applied AI research at Samsung Research Amercia(SRA) and Meta Platforms, Inc., where I built large-scale machine learning and recommendation systems.
